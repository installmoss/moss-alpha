# Moss Alpha — Detailed Workflow

This document expands the research process used by the Moss Alpha skill.

## 1. Noise Assessment

- Check recent mention volume on X
- Look for sudden spikes in social activity
- Prefer tokens with low-to-moderate attention

## 2. On-chain Structure

- Liquidity size and stability
- Top holder concentration
- Number of unique holders and growth rate
- Presence of locked or burned liquidity

## 3. Behavioral Signals

- Pattern of buys (many small buys vs few large ones)
- Consistency of volume over time
- Absence of extreme price candles

## 4. Risk Checklist

- Contract verified?
- High tax or blacklist functions?
- Deployer history
- Liquidity unlock schedule

## 5. Final Framing

Only surface opportunities that pass most of the above filters while remaining relatively quiet.
